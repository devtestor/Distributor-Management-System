# Distributor-Management-System
## System Description

The ** Distributor Management System (BDMS)** is a comprehensive web and mobile platform designed to automate and manage every aspect of a beverage distribution business. Rather than focusing only on inventory, the system provides complete visibility into stock, customer orders, deliveries, empty bottles and crates, finances, debts, expenses, and overall business performance.

The platform enables distributors to efficiently manage products received from BRALIRWA, distribute them to retailers and wholesalers, monitor warehouse operations, reconcile driver deliveries, track customer payments, and generate accurate financial and operational reports. The system is designed for multiple user roles, including owners, administrators, warehouse managers, sales representatives, drivers, and accountants. Each user has access only to the functions relevant to their responsibilities through a secure role-based access control system.

A key feature of the platform is the ability to track returnable assets such as empty bottles and crates, which are critical to beverage distribution businesses. The system records container movements from the warehouse to customers and back, identifies missing containers, calculates their financial value, and assigns responsibility where necessary.

The sales and delivery modules allow sales representatives to create customer orders, generate invoices, record payments, and monitor outstanding customer balances. Drivers can manage deliveries, record returned products and empty containers, reconcile cash collections, and submit delivery reports directly from a mobile device.

The finance module provides complete visibility into business income and expenses by tracking cash, mobile money, bank payments, supplier purchases, operating expenses, and customer credit. It automatically calculates gross and net profit, enabling management to understand the true financial performance of the business.

An interactive owner dashboard provides real-time insights into daily sales, stock levels, low-stock alerts, customer debts, empty container balances, delivery performance, driver reconciliations, product profitability, and overall business health. Comprehensive reporting tools allow users to generate operational and financial reports for informed decision-making.

The system supports multilingual operation, including English, French, Kinyarwanda, and Swahili, making it suitable for distributors operating across the East African region. Mobile users can continue working even in areas with poor internet connectivity through offline capabilities that synchronize data once a connection becomes available.

## Key Objectives

* Digitize beverage distribution operations.
* Improve inventory accuracy and stock visibility.
* Reduce losses caused by missing bottles and crates.
* Control customer credit and outstanding debts.
* Improve delivery monitoring and driver accountability.
* Automate financial tracking and profitability analysis.
* Generate accurate operational and financial reports.
* Support multilingual users and offline field operations.

## Core Business Question

The system is designed to answer one critical business question at any moment:

> **"Where is my stock, where is my money, who owes me, who has my crates, and am I making a profit?"**
