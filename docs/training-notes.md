# Basic User Training Notes

## Owner

- Start every day on Dashboard.
- Watch low-stock, credit exposure, empty container exposure, and active deliveries.
- Use Reports for sales, stock valuation, debt, and empty container review.
- Review audit logs after sensitive changes.
- Deactivate demo or former staff accounts from Settings.

## Admin

- Create staff accounts and assign the correct role.
- Maintain products, prices, reorder levels, and active/inactive status.
- Keep vehicle, route, and customer data clean before daily operations.
- Reset passwords from Settings when required.

## Warehouse Manager

- Receive BRALIRWA stock when products arrive.
- Load trucks through the delivery workflow.
- Record returned and damaged stock during reconciliation.
- Use stock counts for physical inventory checks.

## Salesperson

- Create customer invoices.
- Record cash, mobile money, or bank payments.
- Check customer balance before allowing credit.
- If internet is unstable, save drafts and sync when online.

## Driver

- Review assigned delivery trips.
- Confirm delivered, returned, and damaged quantities.
- Record cash collected and credit issued during reconciliation.
- Keep the offline sync indicator visible when working in the field.

## Accountant

- Review payments and debt reports.
- Export reports to CSV for daily reconciliation.
- Compare cash collected against expected delivery collections.
- Escalate unresolved variances to the owner.
